**Last updated: November 1, 2026.**

This policy describes how information related to the Terra Vasta website is handled. The site is currently a static publication hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not require an account, provide a contact form or newsletter signup, or use its own audience analytics or advertising tools.

## Browsing and hosting data

To deliver and protect the site, GitHub Pages may log and store visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub processes this information as the hosting provider under its [privacy policies](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). See also GitHub’s documentation on [data collected by GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages).

The project does not set analytics or advertising cookies. The light or dark theme preference is saved in your browser’s local storage so the interface can remember your choice. This preference is not sent to the site with each visit and can be cleared through your browser settings.

Search runs on the site using public files published with its pages; we do not use a third-party search service to record your queries.

## Contact messages

If you email [dragaodebotas.studio@gmail.com](mailto:dragaodebotas.studio@gmail.com), we receive the email address you use, any name you provide, and the content and attachments you choose to include. We use this information to reply to and follow up on your request. Please do not send sensitive information that is not needed for your inquiry.

Messages are transmitted and stored by email providers, including Google, under those providers’ policies. Correspondence may be kept for as long as needed to handle the inquiry and meet any legal obligations. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the information you sent by writing to the address above, subject to applicable obligations.

## External links

The site links to third-party services such as GitHub, Ko-fi, Bluesky, and X. When you visit those services, their own privacy practices and policies apply.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the site’s features or practices change. The current version date appears at the top of the page. If the site adds forms, a newsletter, analytics tools, or other features that process data, this policy will be revised to describe them.

For privacy questions or requests, email [dragaodebotas.studio@gmail.com](mailto:dragaodebotas.studio@gmail.com).
